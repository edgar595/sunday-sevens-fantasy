    How it works

    Pick five players from Wazee and Vijana, stay under budget, and score points from what they do across Sunday’s four games.

    Building your team

    • Five players: defenders, midfielders and forwards in the formation you choose. It’s 5-a-side, so there are no keepers.
    • £32.0m to spend.
    • No more than three players from the same side, so you need both Wazee and Vijana in your team.
    • Choose a captain. Their points count double.
    • Each Sunday is one gameweek. Changes lock when the first game starts, and after that they count from the next Sunday.
    • Players score for whichever side they actually played for that day, and only for the games they were on the pitch. If one of your players doesn’t turn up, they score zero.
    • Goals, assists and cards count for the whole Sunday.
    • After each Sunday, everyone votes for the best player. The top three get 3, 2 and 1 bonus points. Voting closes Monday at 21:00. If fewer than 5 people vote, the admin picks the bonus.
